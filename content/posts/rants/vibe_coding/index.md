> **Unpopular opinion:** LLMs are the worst thing that has happened to software development, and we will all pay the price.

## Not about vibe-coders

I'm not talking about "vibe-coders". I don't think that phenomenon deserves serious discussion.

I'm talking about people who use AI as a coding *assistant*, convinced they're still in control of their architecture and design decisions. In reality, the LLM is making those decisions for you, while keeping you under the **illusion of control**.

## You're replacing yourself

LLMs won't take your job. **You're taking your own job by being lazy.** :)

In a couple of years, people who still know how to write proper code will be *very* rich and in demand. And I don't mean complicated, data-critical systems. No, no. Just proper, good old object-oriented code.

## What LLMs actually are

**LLMs are not AGI, and they never will be.**

They are **excellent tools for function approximation and pattern recognition**. They don't *think*. They don't *reason*. They don't *understand*.

## So use them, but use them right

LLMs are useful, just not in the way tech bros and token sellers portray them. Make use of them, and stop following the hype blindly.
